## 1. Executive Summary

**Fudari** is a technology platform that connects customers with verified artisans — electricians, plumbers, mechanics, carpenters, and 20+ other trade categories — across Kenya and, ultimately, across Africa.

Africa's informal skilled-trades sector employs an estimated 50 million people and generates over USD 200 billion annually, yet it remains one of the least digitized industries on the continent. Customers have no reliable way to find, vet, or book a qualified artisan. Artisans have no tools to grow their businesses, manage their reputation, or access steady work.

Fudari solves both sides of this market with a mobile-first platform that offers:

- **For customers:** Instant artisan discovery, AI-powered matching, no-login booking, real-time tracking, escrow-protected payments, and a transparent trust system.
- **For artisans:** A digital storefront, subscription-based ranking for visibility, lead analytics, AI-driven pricing tools, demand forecasting, and direct M-Pesa earnings.

The platform is fully built and operational with **90+ API endpoints, 10 AI features, real-time chat, an escrow payment system, and a 3-tier subscription model** generating recurring revenue from day one.

We are raising **USD 500,000 in seed funding** to launch commercially in Nairobi, acquire our first 10,000 artisans, and prove unit economics before expanding to Mombasa, Kisumu, and two additional East African markets within 24 months.

## 2. Problem Statement

### The Customer's Problem

Finding a reliable artisan in Kenya today is an exercise in frustration:

| Pain Point | Impact |
|---|---|
| **No discovery channel** | 89% of artisans are found through word-of-mouth only |
| **No verification** | Customers cannot verify skills, criminal records, or certifications before inviting someone into their home |
| **No price transparency** | Overcharging is one of the top 3 customer complaints; quotes vary 200–400% for the same job |
| **No accountability** | If work is poor, there is no recourse — artisans vanish after payment |
| **No scheduling** | Coordination happens via WhatsApp/SMS chains, leading to no-shows and delays |

### The Artisan's Problem

Kenya's 3.5 million "Jua Kali" (informal sector) workers face equally severe barriers:

| Pain Point | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Inconsistent work** | Income fluctuates 40–70% month-to-month |
| **Zero digital presence** | 95%+ have no website, Google listing, or social media page |
| **No growth tools** | No data on customer demand, pricing benchmarks, or market trends |
| **Trust deficit** | Skilled artisans are indistinguishable from unskilled ones — reputation is hyper-local |
| **Payment friction** | Cash-only jobs lead to disputes, delayed payments, and no financial records |

### The Market Gap

Existing solutions — WhatsApp groups, Facebook Marketplace, Jiji classifieds — are general-purpose. None provide:

- Structured artisan vetting
- Skill-specific search and ranking
- Payment protection
- AI-driven matching and pricing
- Subscription-based growth tools for artisans

**Fudari is purpose-built for this gap.**

## 3. Solution — The Fudari Platform

Fudari is a two-sided marketplace with three user roles:

```
┌─────────────┐          ┌─────────────────┐          ┌─────────────┐
│  CUSTOMERS  │◄────────►│    Fudari       │◄────────►│  ARTISANS   │

│  - Pay      │  Review   │  Chat • Rank    │  Rank    │  - Wallet   │
│  - Rate     │          │                 │          │  - Analytics│
└─────────────┘          └────────┬────────┘          └─────────────┘
                                  │
                         ┌────────▼────────┐
                         │     ADMIN       │
                         │  - Approvals    │
                         │  - Moderation   │
                         │  - Analytics    │
                         │  - Disputes     │
                         └─────────────────┘
```

### Core Value Propositions

**For Customers:**
- Book an artisan in under 60 seconds — **no login required**
- Every artisan is ranked and scored by a transparent 5-component algorithm
- Payments are held in escrow and released in stages (materials at start, labour at completion)
- AI recommends the best-match artisan based on proximity, trust score, and value
- Track job progress in real-time with PIN verification at each stage

**For Artisans:**
- Create a professional digital storefront with listings, portfolio, and reviews
- Upgrade subscription tier to rank higher in search results and earn more leads
- Access AI-powered pricing tools, demand forecasting, and lead analytics
- Receive direct M-Pesa payments with full transaction history
- Build a verified reputation that travels beyond word-of-mouth

### 5.3 Artisan Ranking System

The ranking system is the core monetization lever. Search results are ordered by a **composite score (0–100)**:

| Component | Weight | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription Tier** | 40% | PRO = 100, BASIC = 60, FREE = 40 |
| **Ratings & Reviews** | 20% | Average rating (0–70) + review volume bonus (0–30) |
| **Performance** | 15% | Completion rate (0–50) + volume (0–30) + on-time (0–20) |
| **Engagement** | 15% | Profile views (0–40) + call/WA clicks (0–40) + listing views (0–20) |
| **Recency** | 10% | Days since last activity (1 day = 100, 30 days = 30) |

**Visibility by tier:**

| Tier | Monthly Price | Search Position | Featured | Crown Badge | Max Listings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FREE** | KES 0 | Lower results | No | No | 1 |
| **BASIC** | KES 500 | Improved | No | No | 3 |
| **PRO** | KES 3,000 | Top results + featured section | Yes | Yes | Unlimited |

This creates a natural upgrade funnel: artisans see their ranking score breakdown and exactly how many views/leads they're missing by not upgrading.

### 5.4 Trust & Safety System

| Layer | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **3-Level Vetting** | STANDARD (no docs) → VERIFIED (National ID + Good Conduct Certificate) → PRO (+ TVET Certification) |
| **AI Trust Score** | 0–100 composite with NLP sentiment analysis on reviews, tier labels (Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze/New) |
| **Badges** | 8+ earned badges: Top Rated, Reliable Pro, Always On Time, Zero Disputes, etc. |
| **Report System** | 7 report reasons, admin moderation pipeline, progressive discipline (Warn → Suspend → Ban) |
| **Fraud Prevention** | 4-digit PIN verification, booking code uniqueness, 1-hour duplicate booking guard, M-Pesa transaction verification |
| **Escrow Protection** | Customer funds held until job milestones are verified |

Fudari operates a **marketplace + SaaS hybrid** model with four revenue streams:

```
Revenue = Subscriptions + Transaction Fees + Lead Monetization + Value-Added Services
```

| Stream | Mechanism | Revenue per Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription Revenue** | Monthly artisan plans (BASIC @ KES 500, PRO @ KES 3,000) | Recurring MRR |
